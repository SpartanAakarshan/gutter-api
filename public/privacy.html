Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

What We Collect

Your Google account email, and an encrypted copy of your API key if you provide one. The text you Alt+Click is sent to the AI provider to generate a response and immediately discarded — we never store it.

What We Don't Collect

We do not track the websites you visit, store your browsing history, read page content beyond what you select, or sell your data to anyone.

Your API Key

If you add your own API key, it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. We cannot read your plaintext key. It is decrypted only in memory at the moment it is used, then released. You can delete it at any time from the extension options.

Third-Party Services

Gutter uses Supabase for authentication, Vercel for hosting, and your chosen AI provider (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or Grok) to generate responses. Each operates under their own privacy policy.

Data Deletion

Remove your API key anytime via extension options. To delete your account entirely, email us and we'll remove all records within 7 days.
